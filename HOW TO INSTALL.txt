QUARTER HOUR TIMESHEET – INSTALL STEPS (about 10 minutes)

A. PUT IT ONLINE (one time)
1. Right-click quarter-hour.zip > Extract All. You get a folder called "quarter-hour".
2. In Microsoft Edge, go to https://app.netlify.com/signup and create a free account
   (signing in first means the site is yours and is not password-locked).
3. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
4. Drag the extracted "quarter-hour" folder onto the page.
5. When it finishes, Netlify shows a web address ending in .netlify.app. Click it.
   Note: your timesheet entries never go to Netlify. They are stored only in Edge on this laptop.

B. INSTALL AS AN APP
6. On that page in Edge, click the "App available" icon at the right of the address bar
   (or ... menu > Apps > Install this site as an app). Click Install.
7. In the dialog that follows, tick "Auto-start on device login".
   To change this later: open edge://apps, click More options on Quarter Hour,
   choose "Auto-start on device login".

C. FIRST RUN
8. In the app, click "Turn on notifications" and choose Allow.
9. Settings > Test mode: you get a prompt every minute for 10 minutes. Check you see the
   Windows notification, hear the sound and can pick a category.
10. If notifications do not appear: Windows Settings > System > Notifications - make sure
    notifications are on, Microsoft Edge is allowed, and Do Not Disturb / Focus is off.

DAILY USE
- Keep the app window open or minimised. Closing it, or the laptop sleeping, stops prompts.
- Missed blocks appear under Catch-up. Tick several and fill them in one go.
- Click once anywhere in the app after it starts each morning so the in-app chime can play.
- Settings > Download backup at least weekly. The app reminds you after 7 days.
